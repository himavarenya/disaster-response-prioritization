# Contributors & Model Authors

The `bert-base-uncased` model is a result of groundbreaking research efforts by the Google AI Language team.
The following individuals have been recognized for their contributions to the development and release of BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers):

## Core Contributors

- Jacob Devlin
- Ming-Wei Chang
- Kenton Lee
- Kristina Toutanova

These contributors are listed as authors in the original [BERT research paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805).

## Supporting Tools & Libraries

The development and deployment of BERT models have been facilitated by several open-source tools and libraries, including:

- [Transformers](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers)
- [TensorFlow](https://www.tensorflow.org/)
- [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/)
- [Hugging Face Hub](https://huggingface.co/)

## Special Thanks

We extend our gratitude to the open-source research community for providing the foundational frameworks and continuous improvements that have made advancements in natural language understanding possible.
