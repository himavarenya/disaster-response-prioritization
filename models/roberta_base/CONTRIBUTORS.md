# Contributors & Model Authors

The `roberta-base` model is a result of groundbreaking research efforts by the Facebook AI Research (FAIR) team.  
The following individuals have been recognized for their contributions to the development and release of RoBERTa (A Robustly Optimized BERT Pretraining Approach):

## Core Contributors

- Yinhan Liu
- Myle Ott
- Naman Goyal
- Jingfei Du
- Mandar Joshi
- Danqi Chen
- Kevin Gimpel
- Andrew McCallum

These contributors are listed as authors in the original [RoBERTa research paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.11692).

## Supporting Tools & Libraries

The development and deployment of RoBERTa models have been facilitated by several open-source tools and libraries, including:

- [Transformers](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers)
- [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/)
- [Hugging Face Hub](https://huggingface.co/)

## Special Thanks

We extend our gratitude to the open-source research community for providing the foundational frameworks and continuous improvements that have made advancements in natural language understanding possible.
